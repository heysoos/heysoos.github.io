Craig Reynolds' flocking model — complex collective motion from three simple local rules applied to each agent: separation, alignment, and cohesion.

> **Controls**
> - Open <span class="ctrl-preview">⚙</span> to tune force weights, perception radius, and max speed (and othe render settings)
> - <span class="pill-preview">Preset</span> drawer — load presets
> - <span class="ctrl-preview"><svg width="11" height="11"><use href="#ic-shader"/></svg></span> shader editor — hot-swap the WGSL render shader at runtime
> - <span class="tab-preview">Audio</span> — bind microphone or audio input to any force parameter
> - <span class="tab-preview">Image</span> — use a webcam, texture, or brush stroke as a background influence layer
> - FPS counter (bottom-left corner) — click to cap or unlock the render rate
